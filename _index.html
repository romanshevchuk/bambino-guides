Cleaning tablet or descaler?

Two different products for two different jobs. They're not interchangeable.

The Bambino only needs descaler. It has no separate tablet cleaning cycle: its clean & descale does both jobs.

The Plus needs both, at different times. The flashing pattern tells you which: see the lights.

Questions

Do I have to buy Breville / Sage ones?

No. Any descaler made for espresso machines works. Breville's own sachet is made by Cafetto (it says so on the pack).

Same for the tablets: any espresso machine cleaning tablet of about 2 g. Breville's come from Cino Cleano.

Don't swap in vinegar, kitchen citric acid or dishwasher tablets.

All lights

Cleaning cycle

Washes old coffee oil out of the group head (where the handle locks in), using a cleaning tablet or sachet.

Due when 1 CUP and 2 CUP take turns flashing, about every 200 shots.

Rinses with the 1 CUP button may count too, so if you rinse before or after each shot, it can come round sooner.

Takes about 5 min.

You need

Hold 1 CUP + 2 CUP only with the machine ON. With it off, that hold resets your shot sizes instead. Did that by accident?

Steps

A few minutes of setup. The machine does the rest.

  1. Fill the handle: the small 1 CUP basket, the rubber disc pressed into the bottom of it, then the tablet (or the sachet's powder) on top. Lock it in as if making coffee.
  2. Empty the drip tray, push it all the way in, and lay the towel under the front. With the disc in, almost no water comes out of the handle: it goes into the tray. So the 2 L container from the manual barely catches anything.

    Tray filling up? The cycle pauses a few times. Slide the tray out during a pause, empty it, push it back. Careful, the water is hot. (Owners' tip, not from the manual.)

  3. Fill the water tank to MAX and push it down firmly.
  4. With the machine on, hold 1 CUP + 2 CUP for 5 s. Both stay lit.
  5. Press 1 CUP or 2 CUP once to start. They take turns flashing while it runs.
  6. Done when the buttons stop flashing and stay lit. Empty and rinse the drip tray, then rinse the handle and disc. Tablet still there? Run it again.

That's it. The light will ask again in about 200 shots.

Questions

Can I clean before the light asks?

Yes, with a different combo, and this one is done with the machine off. Do steps 1–3, turn the machine off (1 CUP + STEAM), then hold 2 CUP + STEAM for 5 s. That opens the same cleaning mode, so press 1 CUP or 2 CUP to start, as in step 5.

Which one is the 1 CUP single wall basket?

The smaller, shallower one with holes across its whole bottom. The dual wall ones say DUAL WALL underneath. The disc is shaped to seal inside the small basket.

Only got dual wall baskets? Older boxes came without single wall ones. Use the 1 CUP dual wall basket: that's what those manuals mean.

Reset your shot sizes by accident?

They're back to the factory amounts: about 30 ml for 1 CUP and 60 ml for 2 CUP. To set your own again:

  1. With the machine on, hold 1 CUP + 2 CUP for 2 s. Both flash.
  2. With coffee in the handle as usual, press 1 CUP to start.
  3. Press 1 CUP again when the cup has enough. That amount is saved.

Same for 2 CUP.

Lost the cleaning disc?

Any 54 mm cleaning disc works (also sold as a blind or backflush disc). Check the size before buying: the common 58 mm ones don't fit.

The light is still flashing after the cycle

Check the cycle ran to the end and that you started it with the machine on.

Owners report that unplugging for ~10 minutes sometimes clears a stuck alert. That's forum advice, not from the manual.

DescaleClean & descale

Dissolves limescale inside the machine. The Bambino has no separate cleaning cycle: this one does both.

Due when 1 CUP + STEAM take turns with 2 CUP.

Takes about 25 min. Just made coffee? Add up to 20 min for it to cool first.

Due when 2 CUP keeps blinking.

Takes about 25 min.

You need

Leave the tank and drip tray in while it runs. Pulling them out mid-cycle can let out hot steam. The tank only comes out at the pause, to refill.

Both cleaning and descale due? Descale first, rinse the tank, then run the cleaning cycle with fresh water.

Steps

You're only needed at the start, once halfway to refill, and at the end.

  1. Turn the machine off (1 CUP + STEAM).

    Just made coffee? It needs to cool first. Do the next two steps meanwhile, then check the metal around the handle feels cool (up to 20 min). Already cold? Carry straight on.

  2. Empty the drip tray and put it back. Set the bowl under the group head and the steam wand.
  3. Take out the water filter if you use one. Fill the tank to the DESCALE line (the 1000 mark) and stir in the powder.
  4. Hold 1 CUP + STEAM for 5 s while it's off. Those two light up.
  5. Press 1 CUP or STEAM to start. They take turns flashing the whole time.
  6. It pauses when the tank runs out. Refill to MAX with plain water, empty the bowl, put both back.
  7. When 1 CUP and STEAM light up again, press either one to start the rinse.
  8. Done when the lights go out (standby). Empty the bowl, rinse and dry the tray and tank, put the filter back.

All done. Back to coffee. Cleaning light flashing too? Cleaning steps

Steps

You're only needed at the start, once halfway to refill, and at the end.

  1. Empty the drip tray and put it back.
  2. Fill the tank to the DESCALE line and stir in the descaling powder.
  3. Set the bowl under the group head and the steam wand.
  4. Hold 2 CUP + STEAM for 5 s to start. 2 CUP flashes the whole cycle.
  5. It pauses when the tank runs out. Rinse the tank, refill to MAX, empty the bowl, put both back.
  6. Press 2 CUP to rinse.
  7. Done when 2 CUP stops flashing. Empty the bowl, rinse and dry the drip tray and tank.

All done. Back to coffee.

Questions

How often should I descale?

Follow the light: the manual gives no fixed schedule. Hard water means more often. Slow flow, weak steam or lukewarm coffee are early signs.

Can I use vinegar or citric acid?

No. Breville says not to use vinegar, and to use only a descaler made for the machine. Kitchen citric acid isn't one either. Breville / Sage descaler, or another espresso machine descaler, is the safe choice.

Steam wand blocked

Dried milk is clogging the tip. Usually it's because the wand wasn't pushed back down after steaming.

The milk lights all blink fast and STEAM is off.

Steps

  1. Push the wand all the way down and check the tank has water and is pushed in.
  2. Hold STEAM for 5 s to blow it clear.
  3. Still flashing? Unscrew the steam tip and clear the holes with the pin tool stored under the water tank. Screw it back on.
  4. Still weak steam after that? Limescale: descale.

Steam's back. Pushing the wand down after each use keeps it clear.
