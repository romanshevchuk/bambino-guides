Something else wrong?

No water comes out / loud pump noise
  1. Tank empty or not pushed all the way down.
  2. Grind too fine, too much coffee or pressed too hard. Try without coffee to check.
  3. Filter basket holes blocked: clear them with the pin tool.
  4. Limescale: descale.
Weak or no steam / wand blocked
  1. Give it a few seconds to heat up.
  2. Check the tank is full and pushed down.
  3. Blow it clear: hold STEAM 5 s3 s. Then unscrew the tip and clear the holes with the pin tool (stored under the water tank).
  4. Still weak: descale.
Coffee leaks around the handle
  1. Turn the handle fully until it resists.
  2. Wipe grounds off the basket rim.
  3. Too much coffee: it pushes against the shower screen.
Espresso only drips out / tastes bitter
  1. Grind a bit coarser.
  2. Use less coffee and trim with the Razor.
  3. Press (tamp) lighter.
Espresso runs too fast / watery / sour
  1. Grind finer.
  2. Use more coffee and tamp firmly.
  3. Stale or pre-ground beans? Use the dual-wall basket.
No crema (the golden foam on top)
  1. Beans too old: use beans 5–20 days after roast, or pre-ground within a week.
  2. Grind finer and tamp firmly.
Coffee not hot enough

Warm everything first: lock in the empty handle and run a shot of water into your cup, then dry the basket. For milk, raise MILK TEMP.

It's on but stopped working

The overheat protection may have tripped. Turn off (1 CUP + STEAM1 CUP + HOT WATER), unplug and let it cool 30–60 minutes. Still dead: call Breville / Sage support.

Too much or too little in the cup

Adjust the grind first. Then set the shot size.

Pulsing / pumping sound

Normal. The pump makes that noise while brewing and steaming.

Water in the drip tray after steaming

Normal. The machine dumps a little water after steaming to reset its temperature. Keep the tray in and empty it often.

How do I turn it off? There's no switch

Press 1 CUP + STEAM together.

Press 1 CUP + HOT WATER together. It also goes to sleep after 10 idle minutes.

No match. Try another word, or check the lights above.

Cleaning cycle

Washes old coffee oil out of the group head (where the handle locks in), using a cleaning tablet.

Due when 1 CUP and 2 CUP take turns flashing, about every 200 shots.
Takes about 5 min.

You need

The machine must be ON. The same button hold while it's off resets your shot sizes instead.

Steps

  1. Put the cleaning disc in the filter basket, the tablet (or sachet powder) on top, and lock the handle into the machine.
  2. Empty the drip tray and put it back. Set the bowl under the handle and the steam wand.
  3. Fill the water tank to MAX and push it down firmly.
  4. Hold 1 CUP + 2 CUP for 5 s. Both stay lit.
  5. Press 1 CUP or 2 CUP once to start. They take turns flashing while it runs.
  6. When the lights go back to normal, it's done. Rinse the handle, disc, drip tray and bowl. Tablet still there? Run it again.
Can I clean before the light asks?

Yes. Turn the machine off (1 CUP + STEAM), then hold 2 CUP + STEAM for 5 s.

The light is still flashing after the cycle

Check the cycle ran to the end and that you started it with the machine on.

Owners report that unplugging for ~10 minutes sometimes clears a stuck alert. That's forum advice, not from the manual.

Breville's website shows STEAM in the button hold; the printed manual says 1 CUP + 2 CUP. If one doesn't work, try the other.

DescaleClean & descale

Dissolves limescale inside the machine. The Bambino has no separate cleaning cycle: this one does both.

Due when 1 CUP + STEAM take turns with 2 CUP after switching on.
Takes about 25 min.

Due when 2 CUP keeps flashing.
Takes about 25 min.

You need

Don't take out or fully empty the tank mid-cycle, and keep the drip tray in. Hot steam can escape.

Steps

  1. Empty the drip tray and put it back. Set the bowl under the group head and the steam wand.
  2. Take out the water filter if you use one. Fill the tank to the DESCALE line and stir in the powder.
  3. Turn the machine off (1 CUP + STEAM) and let it cool completely.
  4. Hold 1 CUP + STEAM for 5 s while it's off. Those two light up.
  5. Press 1 CUP or STEAM to start. They take turns flashing the whole time.
  6. It pauses when the tank runs out. Refill to MAX with plain water, empty the bowl, put both back.
  7. Press 1 CUP or STEAM (whichever is lit) to rinse.
  8. Done when it goes to standby. Empty the bowl, rinse and dry the tray and tank, put the filter back.

Steps

  1. Empty the drip tray and put it back.
  2. Fill the tank to the DESCALE line and stir in the descaling powder.
  3. Set the bowl under the group head and the steam wand.
  4. Hold 2 CUP + STEAM for 5 s to start. 2 CUP flashes the whole cycle.
  5. It pauses when the tank runs out. Rinse the tank, refill to MAX, empty the bowl, put both back.
  6. Press 2 CUP to rinse.
  7. Done when it's back to normal. Empty the bowl, rinse and dry the drip tray and tank.
How often should I descale?

Follow the light: the manual gives no fixed schedule. Hard water means more often. Slow flow, weak steam or lukewarm coffee are early signs.

Water backflush

A quick rinse with plain water to clear grounds and oil after a coffee session. Optional, no tablet. It doesn't replace the cleaning cycle or descaling.

You need

Steps

  1. Empty the drip tray.
  2. Put the cleaning disc in the filter basket and lock the handle in.
  3. Press 1 CUP and let pressure build for about 20 s.
  4. Press 1 CUP again to stop. Water flows back into the drip tray.
  5. Repeat 3 to 4 times.

Daily care

The small stuff that keeps it working. Most of it takes seconds.

After every coffee
Knock out the used coffee puck. Rinse the basket and handle under hot water. Wipe the steam wand and push it down so it clears itself and clear it (point at the drip tray, hold STEAM 3 s).
Every day
Empty and wash the drip tray (sooner if the red float pops up). Wipe the metal shower screen with a damp cloth. Refill with fresh cold tap water.
After a coffee session
Water backflush (optional, 2 min).Lock in the empty handle and press 2 CUP to flush fresh water through.
If steam gets weak
Unscrew the steam tip and clear the holes with the pin tool stored under the water tank.
When a light asks
Cleaning cycle (~200 shots) and descale.Clean & descale when 2 CUP keeps flashing.

Nothing goes in the dishwasher. Not the handle, the drip tray or any other part.

Which water should I use?

Cold tap water. Breville doesn't recommend distilled, demineralised or heavily filtered water: it can affect taste and how the machine works.

Putting it away for a while

Turn it off and unplug it. Empty the tank and drip tray, let it cool and dry, and store it upright with nothing on top.

Espresso

Pick the right basket, then change one thing at a time. Usually that's how fine the coffee is ground.

1. Pick your basket

Single wall
one layer of holes
For fresh beans and a proper espresso grinder. Tastes best, but needs a fine, even grind. The 2-cup one comes fitted.
Dual wall
two layers
For pre-ground or older beans (past ~30 days). More forgiving, a bit less flavour.

2. Make the shot

  1. Run a little water: press 1 CUP briefly with no handle in, to flush and warm up.
  2. Add 16–19 g of coffee to the 2-cup basket (8–11 g in the 1-cup).
  3. Tap to level, then press it flat (tamp) firmly and evenly. Same pressure every time matters more than force.
  4. Twist the Razor tool in the basket to trim the extra coffee. Wipe the rim.
  5. Wipe loose grounds off the rim so it seals.
  6. Lock the handle in, cup on a scale if you have one. Press 2 CUP.
  7. Watch: first drops after 8–12 s, then a flow like warm honey.

3. Taste and adjust

Rule of thumb: twice the coffee out, in 25–30 s. 18 g of coffee → ~36 g of espresso. Not from the manual, but a good target if you have a scale.

Stop a shot early, or pre-infuse by hand

Press the same button again to stop a shot at any time.

Hold 1 CUP or 2 CUP to run low-pressure pre-infusion for as long as you hold. Let go to start the full shot, then press the flashing buttonany button to stop.

Do I need a grinder?

The Bambino has no grinder built in. With pre-ground coffee, use the dual-wall baskets. For single-wall baskets you need a burr grinder that can go espresso-fine. Beans taste best 5–20 days after the roast date.

Set the shot size

Teach a button how much to pour. It remembers until you change it. Out of the box: ~30 ml for 1 CUP, ~60 ml for 2 CUP.

Steps

  1. Hold 1 CUP + 2 CUP for 2 s with the machine on and the handle ready. Both flash.
  2. PressPress and hold for ~3 s the button you want to set (1 CUP or 2 CUP). The shot starts.
  3. Press the same button again when you have enough. That amount is saved.

No input for 30 s, or pressing STEAM / HOT WATER, cancels without saving.

Go back to the factory shot sizes

Turn off (1 CUP + STEAM). Hold 1 CUP + 2 CUP 5 s until lit, then press either one. The lights flash 3 times.

In standby, hold 1 CUP + 2 CUP for 10 s. The lights flash 3 times.

Steam milk

The Plus can do it for you using the sensor under the jug. Or steam by hand like any wand.

By hand. Add a little air at the start, then spin the milk to make it silky.

Automatic

  1. Fill the jug that came with it with cold milk, between MIN and MAX. Auto mode only works with that jug.
  2. Lift the wand, put the jug under, lower the wand fully. The jug sits on the sensor and the milk covers the tip.
  3. Pick MILK TEMP (warm / ideal / hot) and MILK FOAM (low = latte, high = cappuccino).
  4. Press STEAM. It stops by itself at the right temperature.
  5. Lift the wand, take the jug, wipe the tip, lower the wand. It clears itself.

Always put the wand back down after. That's how it cleans itself and doesn't clog. Milk lights stepping down mean it's still up.

By handSteps

  1. Blast the wand into the drip tray for a moment to clear water.
  2. Fill the jug with cold milk, up to where the spout starts.
  3. Tip 1–2 cm under the surface, a bit to the right (3 o'clock). Press STEAM.
  4. Lower the jug slowly until you hear a soft paper-tearing hiss. That's foam being made. Short for latte, longer for cappuccino.
  5. Raise the jug so the tip is just under the surface and the milk spins like a whirlpool.
  6. Stop when the jug is too hot to hold for long (60–65 °C). Press STEAMany button.
  7. Wipe the wand. Lower it so it clears itself.Point it at the drip tray and hold STEAM 3 s to clear it.

Steam keeps hissing a few seconds after you stop. That's normal: wait before pulling the jug away.

Milk too hot, too thin or too bubbly?
  • Big bubbles: the tip was too high. Keep it just under the surface.
  • No foam: the tip was too deep. Let it hiss a little at the start.
  • Too hot in auto: check the jug sits on the sensor, then lower MILK TEMP.
  • Plant milks foam differently. Try "barista" versions and adjust FOAM.
Hot water for an Americano or tea

Point the wand at your cup, lift it, then press 1 CUP + MILK FOAM. Press 1 CUP to stop.

Point the wand at your cup and press HOT WATER. Press any button to stop.
