Descaling steps · 25–45 min

Nothing lights up

Water tank is empty

Top up the water and push the tank down until it sits flush.

Still dark with a full tank? It's switched off. Press any big button.

The two small milk lights step downward

Steam wand is raised

Push the wand all the way down. It cleans itself once it's there.

Milk lights all flash, STEAM is off

Steam wand is blocked

Lower the wand, then hold STEAM for 5 s to blow it clear.

Still blocked?

1 CUP and 2 CUP flash together

Shot size setup is on

Press STEAM to leave. It starts when 1 CUP + 2 CUP are held together.
