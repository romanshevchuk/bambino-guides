Which lights are flashing?

Cleaning reminder

1 CUP and 2 CUP flash in turn

  • 200 shots since the last cleaning cycle.
  • Needs the cleaning disc + one tablet.
  • Not now? Press STEAM. It comes back at next startup.

Descale reminder

1 CUP + STEAM alternate with 2 CUP

  • Shows for about 15 seconds at startup. Limescale build-up.
  • Needs descaling powder and a cooled-down machine.
  • Not now? Press any lit button. That only hides it.
Start descale ↓
Both alerts?Descale first. Then rinse the tank and container and run the cleaning cycle with fresh water. Never reuse descaling water.
Reminder: 1 CUP / 2 CUP

Cleaning cycle (tablet)

Flushes coffee oils from the group head. Tap a step to tick it off.

You needCleaning disc · 1 cleaning tablet · 2 L container
  1. Put the disc in the filter basket, the tablet on the disc, and lock the portafilter in.
  2. Empty the drip tray and put it back. Set the 2 L container under the portafilter and steam wand.
  3. Fill the water tank to MAX and seat it firmly.
  4. With the machine on, hold 1 CUP + 2 CUP for 5 s. Both stay lit.
  5. Press 1 CUP or 2 CUP once. They flash in turn while it runs.
  6. Wait for ready mode. If tablet is left over, run it again. Rinse the portafilter, disc, drip tray and container.
Don't start from OFFThe same hold with the machine off resets your shot volumes.
Clean early (no alert)Machine off: hold 2 CUP + STEAM for 5 s.
Reminder: all three lights

Descale

Removes limescale from the water circuit. Uses descaling powder.

You needDescaling powder · 2 L container · cooled machine
Never remove or fully empty the tank mid-cycle.Take the water filter out first. Keep the drip tray in: hot steam can escape.
  1. Empty the drip tray and put it back. Set the 2 L container under the portafilter and steam wand.
  2. Remove the water filter. Fill to the DESCALE line and stir in the powder.
  3. Turn off (1 CUP + STEAM) and let it cool completely.
  4. With it off, hold 1 CUP + STEAM for 5 s. These two light up.
  5. Press 1 CUP or STEAM to start. They flash in turn all cycle.
  6. It pauses when the solution runs out. Refill to MAX with plain water, empty the container, put both back.
  7. When lit, press 1 CUP or STEAM to resume the rinse.
  8. Done when 1 CUP and STEAM flash in turn and it drops to standby.
  9. Empty the container. Rinse and dry the drip tray and tank. Refit the water filter.
How often?No fixed interval in the manual: follow the alert. Hard water means sooner. Slow flow or weak steam are early signs.

Optional, after use

Clear-water backflush

No tablet. Recommended after each session to clear grounds and oils.

  1. Empty the drip tray.
  2. Put the cleaning disc in the filter basket and lock the portafilter in.
  3. Press 1 CUP and let pressure build for about 20 s.
  4. Stop the shot so water flows back into the drip tray.
  5. Repeat 3 to 4 times.
Timings not confirmedSage shows these steps only in a video. The 20 s and 3 to 4 repeats are from a secondary summary.

Backflush, tablet cycle and descale each do a different job. None replaces another.

Routine care

Baskets & portafilterRinse under hot water right after use.
Shower screenWipe with a damp cloth. Now and then run hot water through with an empty basket.
Steam wandWipe after each use and lower it so it auto-purges. Blocked tip: unscrew it, clear holes with the tool under the water tank.
Drip trayEmpty and wash daily or when the indicator rises. Not dishwasher safe.
WaterCold tap water. Distilled or demineralised is not recommended.
Alert still showing after the cycle?

Check the cycle ran to the end and that cleaning was started with the machine on. The manual gives no other fix.

Owners report unplugging for about 10 minutes sometimes clears a stuck alert (forum advice, not official).

Milk temp / foam lights acting up?

Lights stepping downward: the steam wand is raised. Lower it so it purges.

Both lit and flashing with STEAM off: the wand is blocked. Hold STEAM 5 s to purge, then clean the tip if needed.

Machine stopped working mid-use?

The thermal cut-out may have tripped. Turn off with 1 CUP + STEAM, unplug, and let it cool 30 to 60 minutes.
