Steam wand blocked

Dried milk is clogging the tip. Usually it's because the wand wasn't pushed back down after steaming.

The milk lights all blink fast and STEAM is off.

Steps

  1. Push the wand all the way down and check the tank has water and is pushed in.
  2. Hold STEAM for 5 s to blow it clear.
  3. Still flashing? Unscrew the steam tip and clear the holes with the pin tool stored under the water tank. Screw it back on.
  4. Still weak steam after that? Limescale: descale.

Steam's back. Pushing the wand down after each use keeps it clear.
